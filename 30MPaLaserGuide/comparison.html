DIY Build Advantages

  • Cost: ~100x cheaper than entry-level industrial
  • Full control over design and modifications
  • Learning platform for water-jet laser technology
  • Upgradeable: higher power laser, better pump
  • Open-source parametric CAD (OpenSCAD)

DIY Build Limitations

  • No CNC integration (manual positioning only)
  • Manual beam-to-jet alignment (6 set screws)
  • CW laser only: larger heat-affected zone than pulsed
  • No safety certification (Class 4 laser hazard)
  • No water filtration/recirculation loop
  • Sapphire nozzle lifetime < diamond (industrial)

Industrial Advantages

  • Turnkey solution with 5-axis CNC
  • Motorized auto-alignment of beam to jet
  • CE certified Class 1 enclosure
  • Proven process recipes for many materials
  • Integrated water treatment and monitoring
  • OEM support, training, spare parts

Industrial Limitations

  • Extremely high cost (300k - 1.5M EUR)
  • Proprietary software and consumables
  • Long lead times (6 - 12 months)
  • Locked down: no user modifications
  • Ongoing maintenance contracts required

Sources: Synova SA (Lausanne, Switzerland) pioneered water-jet guided laser (LMJ) technology based on EPFL research. Pulsar Photonics (Germany) offers the RDX1000 LWJ platform.
