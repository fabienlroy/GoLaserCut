Multi-Pass Advantages

  • Each pass removes 0.2 - 1 mm depending on material and power
  • Water jet flushes debris between passes: clean re-entry
  • Kerf stays constant (no taper buildup): aspect ratio up to 100:1
  • Reduced thermal load per pass: less HAZ even with CW
  • 1 kW CW removes more per pass than 200 W pulsed: fewer passes needed

Multi-Pass Limitations

  • Total cutting time = passes × path length / speed
  • Very deep cuts (>15 mm): jet diverges, debris evacuation harder
  • CW mode: cumulative heat may cause issues on heat-sensitive materials
  • Diamond cutting requires 532 nm (frequency-doubled): not possible at 1070 nm
  • Requires CNC or precise repositioning between passes

Key Performance Factors

Why DIY Cuts Faster

  • 5x more power than a typical Synova (1 kW vs 200 W)
  • CW mode delivers energy continuously, no inter-pulse gaps
  • Higher material removal rate on thick metals and composites
  • 30 MPa pressure provides good debris evacuation

Why Industrial Cuts Better

  • Pulsed laser: peak power >10 kW in ns pulses ablates cleanly
  • Minimal HAZ: critical for semiconductors, medical devices
  • 532 nm option: better absorption in Si, GaAs, metals
  • Motorized alignment keeps beam centered in jet

Nozzle Diameter Effect

  • 50 µm: finest kerf (~60 µm), slowest, needs higher pressure
  • 100 µm: good balance (kerf ~120 µm), our DIY choice
  • 150 µm: faster cuts, wider kerf (~180 µm), lower pressure OK
  • Smaller nozzle = more power density = deeper cuts per pass

Pressure Effect (30 vs 50 MPa)

  • Higher pressure = stiffer jet = better beam guiding over distance
  • Better debris flushing at higher pressures
  • 30 MPa is adequate for most materials up to 5 mm
  • 50+ MPa needed for deep cuts (>5 mm) and hard ceramics
